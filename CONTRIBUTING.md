# Contributing

Corrections and additions are welcome, including from vendors about their own
products.

Two rules:

1. **Every factual claim links to the vendor's own documentation or pricing
   page.** Not a review, not a comparison post, not this list. If the vendor
   does not publish it, the entry says so instead of guessing.
2. **No marketing adjectives.** "Industry-leading", "unmatched", "blazing
   fast" and their relatives get edited out. Say what the thing returns, what
   it costs, and what it cannot do.

Prices carry the date they were read. If you update one, update the date at the
top of the README too.
